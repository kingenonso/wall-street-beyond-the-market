# The redesign (12 October 2026)

The owner approved the mockups ("yes i like it") and said "build". This report covers Part B, the build.

## What a player sees now

### 1. The layout
- The same four parts as before: top bar, sections menu, work area (with "Needs you now"), context rail.
- **Phones** get a bottom bar with five sections (Desk, Market, Work, Empire, More). "More" lists every other page.
- The phone gap is fixed. No page is wider than the screen at 390 pixels.
- A "Skip to the page" link is the first thing the keyboard reaches.

### 2. Terminal feel
- **Command bar** in the top bar ("Search: a company, a page, an action…"). Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac) jumps to it from anywhere.
  - It suggests companies first (code, name, price and today's move), then pages, then actions.
  - Arrow keys move through the list; Enter opens one; Escape closes it.
- **Sortable tables**, with small trend lines of each share's last five closing prices.
- **Live ticker** under the top bar.
- **"Today at a glance"**: every share, grouped by industry and tinted by today's move, always with ▲ or ▼.

### 3. Charts
- **Price with volume**: on each company page, with a choice of a real week (one game quarter), four real weeks (one game year) or everything, and a hover readout.
- **Order book as bars** beside the trading panel.
- **"What you own against the BSX 50"** on Portfolio. It says plainly that the player's line also includes salary, rewards and grants.
- **The money you own by kind**, as one bar.
- **The Economy page's charts** were already there. Its key words now have a "?".

### 4. Plain language
- A **"?"** beside financial words (about 30 of them, from BSX 50 to Covenant).
- It opens one plain line and an example. It stays inside the screen.

### 5. The work screens (all 8 changes)
1. A short list of work: In progress, Ready to start (with filters), Done, Coming up. Each has a **Start** button.
2. A focused screen with one question at a time. The menus are hidden so the player can concentrate.
3. The unit beside each answer box. A warning appears when an answer looks like the wrong unit; it never says the right answer.
4. A calculator:
   - Figures from the accounts can be tapped into it. Two tapped figures never run together.
   - For a percentage answer, the player chooses between "It is already a percentage" and "It is a fraction". Nothing is guessed.
5. **"How to do this"**: the general method, written the same for every player.
   - It is exactly the text the debrief shows afterwards under "The professional method".
   - 126 of the 129 kinds of work have one.
6. Simple status labels and stars: ★ = alone, ★★ = with your empire, ★★★ = in a weekend challenge.
7. The long rules behind a "?".
8. A four-step first-time walkthrough, which can be skipped. It is remembered once done.

### 6. Sound
- On by default at low volume (30 of 100), only for the player's own events:
  - a trade filled;
  - a new message;
  - a decision waiting;
  - the closing bell.
- News is silent. One tap on the speaker in the top bar mutes everything. A volume slider is in the profile menu.

### 7. Light mode
- Dark is the default. Light mode is in the profile menu and is remembered for the player.

### 8. Faster early levels
- Levels 1 to 10 come twice as fast: Level 10 needs 1,350 experience instead of 2,700. That is about two real weeks.
- Levels 11 to 19 close the gap smoothly.
- Level 20 and above are unchanged. The Level 20 gate still needs the rank case.

### 9. Accessibility
- An automatic accessibility check (the standard WCAG AA rules plus best practice) was run on:
  - every page;
  - dark and light;
  - desktop width and phone width;
  - as a new player, and as an empire head with work open.
- **It now finds nothing.** It started with 9 kinds of problem; all were fixed:
  - links inside sentences are underlined;
  - tables that scroll sideways can be reached by keyboard;
  - headings are in order;
  - every market page has a main heading;
  - light-mode gold and green were darkened for contrast;
  - "today at a glance" text is readable on every tint;
  - empty table headers are named;
  - the industries map uses the right roles.
- Every row of tabs now works with the arrow keys, Home and End.

### 10. Speed
- Pages load only what they show:
  - the five closing prices are read once and cached until the next trade;
  - a story's price move reads only the days it needs;
  - the market answer is shared by the pages that use it for 10 seconds;
  - a new database index on trading day.

## What changed underneath
- **Database:** migration `022_redesign.sql`.
  - Adds four settings per player: look, sound on/off, volume, walkthrough done.
  - The database itself refuses a volume outside 0–100 or an unknown look.
  - Adds an index on trading day.
  - **It needs no fresh world.**
- **New route:** `PATCH /api/me/settings`. It can change only those four settings of the player's own account.
- **Additions to existing answers:**
  - the five closes beside each share;
  - each share's name and industry in the ticker;
  - how prices moved after each story;
  - "How to do this";
  - the count of work in hand.

## Checks

### The ten-question loophole check (`tests/test_redesign_loopholes.py`, 10 tests, all pass)
1. Can the settings route change anything but the player's own four settings, or someone else's? **No.**
2. Can "How to do this" give an answer away?
   - **No.** It is the same for every player and holds none of that work's figures.
   - It is only shown when the judging code has exactly one fixed text.
3. Can a story's price move come from a story not yet out, or from a private company? **No.** Both were tested directly.
4. Can the command bar show a private company? **No.**
5. Can the faster levels pass the Level 20 gate, or make anything above Level 10 cheaper than at Level 20? **No.**
6. Can the dots (work, rank, messages) tell a player about anyone else? **No.**
7. Can a bad setting reach the database by another way? **No.** The database refuses it.
8. Can the trend lines show a price that never happened, or a private company's? **No.**
9. Can a piece of work be handed in twice, or by someone else? **No.**
10. Can anyone see another player's settings? **No.** Eight public pages were checked, and each was checked to really answer.

The reviewer's minor points were done too:
- Tabs work with the arrow keys.
- The empire's seat grid no longer shows a made-up count of 13 seats. An empire has no fixed number of seats, so it shows the real members and one "room for more" card.

## Honest limits
- **Existing players move up a few levels at once** when this update arrives, because Levels 1–10 now need less experience.
  - It is the same rule for everyone, so it is fair, and nobody loses anything.
  - Nobody is told by message; they will simply see the higher level.
- **Three kinds of work have no "How to do this":** the three "buy a company" tasks (ce_buy and its two variants). Their method text is built from the work's own figures, so it is held back until after hand-in.
- **The sounds are made by the browser,** not recorded. A browser plays nothing until the player first taps or clicks the page.
