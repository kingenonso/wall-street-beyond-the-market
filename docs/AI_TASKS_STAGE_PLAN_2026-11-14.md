# Stage plan: a thousand tasks, written by an AI and marked by the game

Agreed by the owner on 14 November 2026 ("yes let it different like 1000+ task using an ai that generates this task").
Nothing is built until the owner says "build".

## The rule this stage never breaks

**The AI writes the task; the game marks it.**
- The AI never decides whether an answer is right.
- Every answer is judged by a checker the game already has, against its own records: the ledger, the filed accounts, the
  prices, and what the world then did.
- So a player cannot argue with the AI or trick it, and an AI's slip can never pay or fail anyone.

## How it works

1. **Checkers.** The game already has about 100 ways to judge work: a valuation, a ratio, a forecast, a default call, a
   risk limit, a deal's terms, a story's facts. Each is a marking scheme.
2. **Scenarios.** For each checker the AI writes many situations, each with its own client, story and twist. For
   example, "value a company" becomes:
   - a family selling its brewery;
   - a rival circling a bank;
   - a fintech raising money before listing;
   - a pension fund asking whether to hold or sell.
3. **Live figures.** When a task is given, the game fills it with a real company from that quarter: its figures, its news,
   its outcome. The words are new; the numbers are true.
4. **Fit.** Each scenario says which build it suits, what level it needs, and which kinds of company or events it
   needs. It is only offered when the world has a match: a "bank in trouble" scenario only appears when a bank is in
   trouble.

About 100 checkers × 10 to 20 scenarios each = **1,000 to 2,000 kinds of task**, each different every time it is given.

## Written in advance, in seasons (not live)

- The AI writes a batch (the first about 1,500).
- Every scenario is checked automatically before it ships:
  - it names a real checker;
  - it uses only figures the game provides;
  - it has no made-up numbers;
  - it breaks none of the news wording rules.
- The batch is then saved into the game like any other content.
- Players need no internet or AI account while playing, and nothing reaches a player untested.
- A new batch arrives each season, every 2–3 real months, with a theme (a debt crisis, a tech boom, foreign takeovers)
  and a season-end prize for the top empires.

## Order of work

1. **A trial batch of 100** for the owner to read and judge the quality.
2. **The scenario format**, the automatic checks, and the way the game picks a scenario that fits the world.
3. **The first full batch** (about 1,500), spread across the three builds, the newsroom and the head of an empire.
4. **Seasons:** theme, batch, and the season-end prize in the empire league.
5. **The usual checks:**
   - loophole tests (a player cannot pick an easy scenario, ask again, or see the answer);
   - an independent review;
   - a ten-year run;
   - a report.

## What the owner provides

- An AI service account for writing the batches (Anthropic's or another).
- A budget. A batch of 1,500 costs a few dollars to a few tens of dollars, depending on the model.
